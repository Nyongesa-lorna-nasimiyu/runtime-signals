# Source and citation policy

Date: 2026-08-28

## Evidence standard

Every article identifies a real system problem, names the invariant, explains the failure mechanism, supports consequential claims with direct primary links, gives an implementable practice, and states limitations. Mark inference, opinion, and unresolved claims explicitly during drafting; publication gates reject unresolved markers.

## Source priority

1. Standards, specifications, official documentation, source repositories, and original papers.
2. Maintainer-authored engineering reports and reproducible production write-ups.
3. Benchmark/dataset documentation and code.
4. Credible secondary analysis for discovery or context, never as sole proof for a consequential claim.

Record access date for time-sensitive pricing, limits, APIs, releases, and policies. Recheck before launch and before publishing claims likely to change.

## Citation record

Structured citation fields: source ID, canonical URL, title, publisher, authors, publication date, access date, source class, supports/contradicts relationship, exact claim scope, quoted limits if any, and license notes. The prose may render a citation, but the source record remains the audit object.
